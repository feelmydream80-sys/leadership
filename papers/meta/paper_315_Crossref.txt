Title: The Mediating Role of AI-Driven Leadership Decision Making in the Relationship Between Green AI and Sustainable Leadership Behaviors
Authors: Malik Habib, Jalaludeen Kuzhikkala
Source: Crossref
Date: 2026-5-1
URL: https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-2600-1348-9.ch005

Abstract (EN):
This study focuses on the employment of AI towards Sustainable Leadership Behaviors through its influence on leadership decision making with a focus on environmental welfare. Based on the data gathered from professionals working in the banking industry in the UAE. This paper studies how AI Readiness, AI Tools &amp; Systems, AI Competency, and AI Governance &amp; Ethical Integration impact AI-Driven Leadership Decision Making and subsequently Sustainable Leadership Behaviors. Using quantitative approach and regression based mediation analysis, the results demonstrate that AI enablers play a significant role in improving AI-Driven Leadership Decision Making which positively influences sustainability oriented leadership practices. The results further confirm that AI-Driven Leadership Decision Making is an important mechanism by which AI capabilities are transformed into environmentally responsible leadership behaviors. The significance of this research is the contribution of bridging the gap between AI and the sustainability of the environment by making leadership behaviors as a central link between technology and sustainable outcomes. This research gives a good idea to organizations and policy makers interested in ensuring that AI is aligned to long term ecological health by integrating AI in leadership operations rather than focusing on AI operational efficiency.
