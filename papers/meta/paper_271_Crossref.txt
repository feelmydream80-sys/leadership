Title: Leader and leadership development
Authors: Marian Iszatt-White, Christopher Saunders
Source: Crossref
Date: 2020-5-7
URL: https://doi.org/10.1093/hebz/9780198834298.003.0018

Abstract (EN):
<p>This chapter focuses on the ideas and theories attached to both leader development and leadership development. It highlights the dominance of programmes focused on the development of the knowledge and skills of the leader and considers some ideas of how the development of leadership could be enhanced. The chapter also examines some approaches to leadership learning, including the foundations and application of action learning, coaching and mentoring, experiential learning, and reflection. Social and relational approaches to leadership development and the importance of developing phronesis (practical wisdom) in leadership judgement and decision making are discussed. The chapter concludes by looking at some of the latest thinking on the use of mindfulness to develop leaders.</p>
