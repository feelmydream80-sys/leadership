Title: Servant Leadership
Authors: Chad Hartnell, G. James Lemoine
Source: Crossref
Date: 2025-5-27
URL: https://doi.org/10.1093/obo/9780199846740-0235

Abstract (EN):
<p>Servant leadership is a style of leadership in which leaders (i.e., those who attempt to influence others toward a shared goal) are others-focused and committed to improving the well-being of multiple stakeholders, including employees, teams, organizations, shareholders, customers, and the community. Servant leadership is a moral form of leadership, alongside ethical and authentic leadership, that has gained prominence in the leadership landscape for its distinctive others-first mentality and its ability to influence outcomes even after accounting for other forms of leadership. A rapidly accumulating literature documents servant leadership’s positive effects for multiple stakeholders. Cutting-edge research is focused on explaining how servant leadership emerges, understanding its positive and negative effects on leaders themselves, and refining how servant leadership’s moral foundations influence others’ morality, attitudes, and behaviors.</p>
