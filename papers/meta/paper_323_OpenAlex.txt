Title: AI-Driven Leadership in the Modern Era for Revolutionizing Next-Generation Strategies
Authors: Satya Subrahmanyam
Source: OpenAlex
Date: 2025-01-24
URL: https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3693-7175-6.ch003

Abstract (EN):
This chapter explores the transformative impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on leadership practices in the modern era. It begins with an overview of AI's rapid evolution and its pervasive influence across industries, highlighting its role in reshaping organizational dynamics and fostering a data-driven culture. The concept of AI-driven leadership is defined, emphasizing its distinctive characteristics such as data-centricity, agility, and enhanced decision-making capabilities compared to traditional leadership models. Key topics include AI's role in strategic decision-making, innovation acceleration, and organizational agility. Ethical considerations surrounding AI applications, workforce implications, and future trends are also discussed, underscoring AI's pivotal role in shaping next-generation leadership strategies and organizational success.

AI 애플리케이션, 인력에 미치는 영향, 미래 동향을 둘러싼 윤리적 고려 사항도 논의되어 차세대 리더십 전략과 조직 성공을 형성하는 데 있어 AI의 중추적인 역할을 강조합니다.
