Title: Preparing Educational Leaders for AI Literacy Through Ethical, Purpose-Driven, and Culturally Responsive Leadership
Authors: Rolla Elsaiary
Source: Crossref
Date: 2026-2-20
URL: https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-4908-4.ch005

Abstract (EN):
As artificial intelligence (AI) becomes integrated into instruction, assessment, communication, and school operations, educational leadership must shift from technical adoption to purpose-driven and ethical practice. This chapter argues that effective AI leadership begins with knowing one's “why,” drawing on Simon Sinek's purpose-centered leadership, Brené Brown's focus on vulnerability and courage, and John C. Maxwell's influence-based model. AI literacy is defined as a holistic capacity that combines technical awareness with ethical judgment, cultural responsiveness, equity-focused thinking, and strategic clarity. The L.E.A.D. Framework (Learn, Evaluate, Apply, Develop) is presented as a guide for intentional AI leadership, supported by principles of psychological safety, engagement, and meaningful work. A dual-language, project-based learning example illustrates how human-centered leadership guides responsible AI use. The chapter concludes with practical tools and a research agenda for building ethical, equitable, and future-ready AI leadership capacity.
