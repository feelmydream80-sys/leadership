Abstract (EN):
Healthcare leadership establishes and determines the strengths in collaborative, and adaptive practices between healthcare leaders and followers that enhance the patient-centered outcomes and drive inventions, innovation and modernization. Effective healthcare leaders integrate the new evolving technology—such as telemedicine, and predictive analytics—to enhance the healthcare domain interactions, efficiency, efficacy and improve the decision-making. Many success stories arise where at least in some cases include a 30% rise in prenatal care compliance through new technological advances and digital strategies. Nevertheless, challenges continue as happened in every new systems applications, including digital illiteracy, resistance to change management, new technology. The healthcare leadership models sometimes misaligned with followers and patients expectations. To address these gaps, opportunities lie in advancing digital transformational digital leadership, data-driven decision on making and equity strategies, and situational development programs.
