Title: From Data To Decisions
Authors: Tendai Charles, Abdulai Abukari, Hend Al Mazmi
Source: Crossref
Date: 2026-2-20
URL: https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-4908-4.ch013

Abstract (EN):
This chapter examines the emergence of AI-driven pedagogical leadership as a critical response to the challenges and opportunities posed by the integration of artificial intelligence in education. Set against the backdrop of increasingly data-intensive digital ecosystems, it argues that effective leadership must move beyond technological adoption to cultivate the capacity for ethical, inclusive and contextually grounded decision-making. Drawing on theories of distributed leadership, sensemaking and data use, the chapter explores how AI tools can support strategic planning, staff development and resource allocation. It critically analyses ethical concerns, risks of technocentrism and the erosion of teacher autonomy, while highlighting persistent data literacy and infrastructure gaps. The chapter concludes by proposing models for capacity building and strategic recommendations for embedding AI within continuous improvement cycles.
